## 🎮 About the Game

Yet Another Zombie Survivors is a single-player 3D bullet-heaven roguelite developed by Awesome Games Studio. It entered Early Access on July 13, 2023, and reached Version 1.0 on August 20, 2026. Players lead a squad of survivors through zombie-infested maps, combining automatic weapons, abilities, upgrades, objectives, and long-term progression.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Yet Another Zombie Survivors**. This trainer adds optional quality-of-life and accessibility controls for runs in Yet Another Zombie Survivors. It can protect the squad, accelerate progression, improve damage output, control game speed, and simplify resource collection while preserving the game's movement, automatic attacks, survivor recruitment, level-up choices, and map objectives.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more accessible, relaxed, experimental, or time-efficient way to experience the game's survivor squads, weapon evolutions, abilities, missions, maps, and Boss Rush mode.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Squad Health** — Prevents the active survivor squad from losing health during a run.
- **Adjustable Game Speed** — Changes the speed of the current run for faster farming or more relaxed play.
- **Magnet Pickup Range** — Collects nearby experience gems and compatible pickups without requiring precise movement over each item.
- **Freeze Mission Timer** — Pauses the active run timer while allowing the squad to continue moving and fighting.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ability Charges** — Keeps survivor abilities available as their cooldowns are completed.
- **One-Hit Kills** — Allows squad weapons and abilities to eliminate standard enemies with a single successful hit.
- **No Cooldowns** — Removes waiting periods between supported automatic attacks and active abilities.

### 📌 Progression

- **Unlimited Experience** — Provides continuous experience gain from defeated enemies and collected experience gems.
- **Instant Level Ups** — Advances the squad to the next level immediately, enabling faster access to weapon, ability, and stat upgrade choices.
- **Unlimited Run Currency** — Keeps run-related currency available for supported upgrade and progression interactions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move the squad with WASD or the controller's left stick. Movement is the primary direct control because survivors automatically aim and fire according to their equipped weapons and abilities. Keep the group moving around enemy packs, collect experience gems and pickups by walking over them, and use the trainer's game-speed or health options only when needed.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional inventory filled with manually equipped loot. Each survivor brings a weapon and abilities into the run, while level-up screens offer new abilities, passive bonuses, weapon improvements, and evolutions. Chest rewards and progression choices are handled through dedicated menus rather than an item grid.

### World & Environment

Runs take place on zombie-filled maps with escalating enemy waves, pickups, objectives, elite enemies, and bosses. Start with one survivor, answer SOS calls to recruit up to two additional survivors, and build a three-person squad with complementary weapons and abilities. The hub, missions, unlocks, challenges, and training progression provide advancement between runs.

### Combat & Weapons

Survivors attack automatically while the player controls positioning and chooses upgrades. Defeat zombies to collect experience gems, level up, and select improvements for weapons, abilities, movement, defense, and other statistics. Use area attacks against dense hordes, maintain distance from elite enemies and bosses, and combine compatible upgrades to develop stronger weapons and abilities.
